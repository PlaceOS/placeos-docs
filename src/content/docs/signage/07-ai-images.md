---
title: Making Artwork with AI
---

Signage Manager can generate poster artwork from a written brief, and change
artwork you already have. It is off until an administrator adds an AI provider,
and the buttons stay hidden until then.

## Making a new image

Open the media library and choose **Create image with AI** (the sparkle button
in the header, or the same entry in the add menu on a small screen).

Describe what the image should show, in plain words:

> a warm poster for our office Christmas party on Friday 10 December

Choose the shape (16:9 for a landscape screen, 9:16 for a portrait one) and how
many options you want, then **Generate**. The options come back in a few
seconds. Pick the one you like.

Behind the scenes the brief is combined with your organisation's brand kit, so
the colours and tone match the rest of your signage without you having to
describe them each time.

### Why the image comes back with no words on it

By default the picture is generated as a background with a clear area at the
top, and you type the headline over it afterwards. No image model spells
reliably at small sizes, and a logo a model has drawn is a redrawing rather
than your actual logo. Typing the words yourself keeps them sharp, correct and
editable later.

If you would rather the model rendered the words, turn off **Add the words
myself afterwards** before generating.

## Asking for changes

Under the options there is a box to ask for a change:

> make the background darker and add a few paper decorations

Each change is applied to the picture you have selected, and the earlier
versions stay on the rail above, so you can always go back to one you liked.
Ask for as many changes as you need. Every instruction so far is carried along
with the request, so the picture keeps building rather than starting over.

## Adding the words and your logo

Choose **Add the words** to open the layer editor. Type a headline and a second
line, choose where they sit and which brand colour they use, and turn the
shaded band on or off if the picture is busy behind the text.

If your brand kit has a logo, **Show our logo** places the real logo file over
the artwork, in whichever corner you choose. The generated picture was asked to
leave that corner clear.

**Save** writes the finished poster into the media library like any other
image, so it can be added to playlists, scheduled, and approved the usual way.

## Changing an image you already have

Any image in the library can be sent back through the same flow: open its menu
and choose **Change image with AI**, then describe what should be different.
The result is saved as a new item, so the original is untouched.

## Limits

- Each person has a daily allowance and each domain a monthly one. What you
  have left is shown under the brief.
- Requests that the vendor's safety system refuses come back with an
  explanation. Rewording usually clears it.
- Options you do not keep are removed after 48 hours. Anything you saved into
  the library stays.
- Generated images are marked in the library with an `ai-generated` tag.
